# Residential Home Kitchen — 3D Asset Staging

This is separate from the commercial restaurant kitchen collection. It is intended for an ordinary family home kitchen: fitted cabinets, countertop, sink, fridge, cooker, hood, microwave, small appliances and optional breakfast/dining furniture.

## Free model sources selected

### 1. Fitted Kitchen and Bathroom Builder — CC0
- Pack: https://3dassets.dev/packs/fitted-kitchen-and-bathroom-builder
- Manifest: https://3dassets.dev/api/v1/packs/fitted-kitchen-and-bathroom-builder
- Starter scene: https://cdn.3dassets.dev/assets/17931/v1/model.glb
- 132 modular GLB models, 6.7 MB total.
- Includes base, wall and tall kitchen units on a 300–1000 mm grid, cabinet fronts, worktops, trims and islands, plus bathroom fixtures.
- CC0 1.0: free for commercial use, no attribution required.
- Prefer importing individual parts from the manifest so cabinets, doors, worktops and appliances can be changed independently.

### 2. Vinish.Dev — Kitchen appliances and cabinets — CC BY 4.0
- Pack: https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/kitchen
- 14 models in GLB, glTF, OBJ and STL; 823 KB ZIP.
- Includes fridge freezer, gas range cooker, microwave, sink with mixer tap, base cabinet, wall cabinet, cooker hood, washing machine, toaster, kettle, espresso machine, blender, saucepan/frying pan and cutting board.
- Real-size models; free for personal and commercial use.
- Attribution required: " [model name] " by vinish.dev (https://vinish.dev/free-3d-models/), CC BY 4.0.

### 3. Realistic Kitchen Free 3D Model — Sketchfab — CC0
- Model page: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/realistic-kitchen-free-3d-model-cc0-a099df104f4c43ec853acc156a1fce87
- Single complete kitchen scene, original GLB.
- Creator states CC0; review the download page and included licence before importing.

### 4. Free Kitchen & Dining Objects — Render a House — no attribution
- Pack page: https://www.renderahouse.com/blog/free-kitchen-and-dining-3d-models
- 11 models with GLB, FBX and OBJ: kitchen run, dining table, chair, fridge, sink, coffee machine, bar stool, chimney/hood, microwave, oven and stove.
- Real-world meter scale; free for personal and commercial project use, no attribution.
- Restriction: do not resell or redistribute the files as a standalone pack or another model library.

## Target folder contents

- `cabinet-modules/` — base, wall, tall and corner cabinets; doors and drawers
- `worktops-backsplashes/` — counters, islands, splashbacks and trims
- `appliances/` — refrigerator, cooker, hob, oven, hood, dishwasher, microwave
- `sink-taps/` — sink bowls, drainers, mixer taps and accessories
- `small-appliances/` — kettle, toaster, coffee machine, blender
- `kitchen-decor/` — jars, utensils, dish racks, plants and countertop accessories
- `breakfast-dining/` — breakfast table, stools, dining table and chairs
- `materials/` — cabinet finishes, wood, stone, quartz, tile and metal textures
- `licenses/` — licence copies, creator credits and source records

## Import and quality rules

1. Keep each downloadable GLB/GLTF model separate wherever possible.
2. Preserve original filenames and add a source/licence record for every asset.
3. Check the model's scale, UVs, material maps and texture appearance in Blender before accepting it.
4. Prioritise realistic residential finishes: wood veneer, matte lacquer, quartz/stone worktops, brushed metal, ceramic and glass.
5. Keep doors, drawers, handles, appliances and worktops independently replaceable where the source supports it.
6. Do not integrate into the live website until the models have been reviewed.
