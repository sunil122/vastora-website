## Staging folders

- `furniture/` — sofas, chairs, beds, tables, wardrobes and storage
- `kitchen-dining/` — cabinets, appliances, dining sets and kitchen props
- `bathroom/` — basins, taps, showers, toilets, tubs and accessories
- `lighting-electrical/` — chandeliers, pendants, lamps, switches and sockets
- `flooring-surfaces/` — parquet, marble, tiles, stone and wall finishes
- `decor/` — carpets, curtains, mirrors, art and accessories
- `licenses/` — source and attribution records

## Import rules

1. Keep the original GLB/GLTF file intact when first importing.
2. Record source URL, creator, licence, download date and any modifications.
3. Do not add an asset to the live website until it has been reviewed for quality, scale, materials and licence.
4. Prefer individual GLB files over a combined room scene so every product can later be selected and replaced independently.
5. These sources are free to download; no paid generation or asset purchase is authorised.
